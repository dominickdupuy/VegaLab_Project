## Definition

The simulator prices every contract at `sigma + iv_premium_vol_pts / 100` and drives the path with `sigma` (`optspread/market.py`), so the planted premium is implied vol less the vol that then drives the path. The real analogue, per day t:

    premium_t = 100 * (IV30_t - RV_t / c_21)
    RV_t      = sqrt(252 / 21 * sum_{i=1..21} r_{t+i}^2),  r = close-to-close log return

- **Horizon.** 21 trading days, the simulator's front tenor (`MarketConfig.tenor_days` = (21, 42)), against the panel's 30-calendar-day ATM IV; 252 days a year, `trading_days_per_year`.
- **Zero mean.** The simulated path has no drift and a hedged seller is paid on squared returns, so RV is a root mean square, not a standard deviation.
- **Jensen.** A vol over 21 Gaussian returns reads `c_21 = 0.9882` times the true vol on average, so `IV - RV` overstates the planted premium; dividing by `c_21` removes it (+0.18 vol points on average here).
- **Spot.** The panel's `spot`: the vendor close to 2025-08-29, then the parity prepaid forward.

## Reading a power curve at it

`optspread.vrp.power_at_noncentrality(premiums, noncentrality, n_seeds, estimate, interval)` reads the M4 curve from its knots through the analysis's own interpolation (noncentrality linear in premium, exact noncentral t) and returns detection probability at the headline, at both interval ends and its range over the interval; a curve that dips is read as it is. `optspread.vrp.power_at(premiums, power, ...)` reads sampled powers linearly. `VRP_MAPPING.json` carries the inputs under `power_inputs`.

## Not settled here

- The simulator plants a constant premium at every strike and tenor; the real one moves with the regime (by year above) and is larger in the put wing than at the money. This maps the at-the-money, 30-day premium only.
- Section 5.5's position size, multiplier and holding period do not enter a premium in vol points; what a defined-risk structure harvests of it is what the sweep measures, so it is in the power curve, not here.
